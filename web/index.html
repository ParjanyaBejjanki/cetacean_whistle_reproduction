A reproduction of VAE-GMM — a model that learns the shapes of false killer whale whistles well enough to imagine new ones.

Scroll ↓

Out here,
a name is a sound.

In bottlenose dolphins, a learned signature whistle works like a name. This model learned from 180,154 whistle contours of false killer whales, recorded off Hawai‘i in 2017.

01 · Signal

Hold a whistle still,
and it becomes a line.

Pressure over time turns into pitch over time — a frequency contour.

02 · Model

Many calls.
A handful of shapes.

A variational autoencoder compresses each contour into a point in latent space. A Gaussian mixture finds the clusters.

03 · Generate

Learn the shape well enough,
and a new call can be imagined.

Pick a cluster, decode a contour, synthesise the sound.

Cetacean Acoustic Lab
Signal Model Generate

Light fades first.
The voices don’t.

A study of dolphin whistle contours — and of a model that learns their shapes well enough to imagine new ones.

Out here,
a name is a sound.

Among dolphins, a learned whistle can work as a name.

01 · Signal

Hold a whistle still,
and it becomes a line.

Pressure over time turns into pitch over time — a frequency contour.

02 · Model

Many calls.
A handful of shapes.

A variational autoencoder compresses each contour into a point in latent space. A Gaussian mixture finds the clusters.

03 · Generate

Learn the shape well enough,
and a new call can be imagined.

Pick a cluster, decode a contour, synthesise the sound.

Synthetic · 1.8 s · not a field recording

heard differently.
